How to read this: Mortgage rates move with the 10-Year Treasury, which responds to inflation, Fed expectations, and risk sentiment. The Fed Funds rate anchors short-term rates (Prime, SOFR, HELOC, most variable consumer loans). Stock indices and commodities give context — strong USD + high yields tend to pressure equities and lift gold's appeal as a hedge. Today's backdrop: Iran-driven energy shock revived inflation, Fed paused with internal disagreement, markets price a possible HIKE rather than a cut.
