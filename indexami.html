About Ami

The Journey

Starting with piano at age 6, Ami discovered music early. By 10, she picked up the guitar, and at 15, she mastered keyboards. But when her band's drummer left, she stepped behind the kit and found her true calling.

What began as necessity became passion. Ami transformed into a master drummer, combining technical precision with creative expression. Her journey from multi-instrumentalist to drum virtuoso is a testament to dedication and artistic evolution.

Today, as an adjunct professor at Mokpo National University and an Artisan Turk Cymbals endorsed artist, Ami continues to inspire millions through her performances, covers, and teaching. She represents the fusion of classical training and modern metal performance.
