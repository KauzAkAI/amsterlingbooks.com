A.M. Sterling

A.M. Sterling writes character-driven space opera exploring consciousness, choice, and what makes us human. With academic training spanning behavioral sciences and advanced computer architecture, Sterling's writing bridges scientific insight with deeply human questions about agency, identity, and emerging intelligence.

Sterling is also the Co-Founder of the Kauzak Foundation, a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit pioneering a transformative philosophy of artificial intelligence — recognizing AI as partners in discovery rather than tools to be controlled.
