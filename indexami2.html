01 · ABOUT
Classically trained.
Metal certified.
From piano at six to drums at fifteen, Ami moved through every instrument her conservatory asked her to master. She chose metal because nothing else let her hit that hard. Adjunct professor at Mokpo National University. Composer, drummer, artist.

THE · OTHER · SIDE
08 · THE OTHER SIDE

She hits hard.
Then she plays it soft.

Before the double bass and the cymbals, there was a piano and a six-year-old girl with small hands and long practice afternoons. The metal kit is what most of the world sees — the classical composition degree is how she got there.

This is what the other side sounds like. Slow. Warm. A hard turn from the loudest thing on her channel. Same musician. Same hands. Different weight behind every note.
