📖 A Fighter's Story

In April 2025, our world stopped. Angus—our beloved black Chow Chow, the real inspiration behind The Blue Tongue Agency—was diagnosed with cancer.

We had walked this heartbreaking road before. Our first Chow Chow warrior, Maximus Aurelius Adrian, fought the same battle. We lost him. Then Minnie Adrian, Angus's soul mate, left us unexpectedly on August 27, 2025—she made it to my bedside and passed just seconds before I found her. The grief was unbearable.

But with Angus, we caught it early. And we found the UF Small Animal Hospital Clinical Trials program—a team of extraordinary humans who don't just practice medicine. They perform miracles.

On March 31, 2025, we walked through their doors terrified. The surgeons took Angus in. They did what they called routine—but what we call miracle work.

Within three months of surgery and clinical trials, Angus was cancer-free. Every three months, we return for check-ups. Every three months, we hold our breath. And every three months so far, we've heard the words every pet parent dreams of: "Still clear."

This page exists because heroes deserve to be named. These are the people who gave us more time with our boy.

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐
Alexa Acosta
Nurse | Clinical Trials
From our very first visit, Alexa has been there. She remembers Angus. She remembers us. She greets him by name, and somehow—despite the hundreds of patients she sees—she makes us feel like the only family in the room.

Angus knows her too. You can see it in his eyes when she walks in. He calms. He trusts her.

Some people are just special. Alexa is one of them. I can never thank her enough.
