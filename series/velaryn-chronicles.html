Descend

In the year 2359, the Velaryn Sovereignty — a forty-seven system interstellar nation governed by a beloved royal dynasty — is thrown into crisis when its king and queen are killed in what appears to be a fold-drive accident.

Their daughters, Kelsey and Lauren, inherit twin thrones at the worst possible moment: the corrupt senate is poised to seize power, the galaxy's most dangerous empire is preparing for war, and the truth behind their parents' deaths is more devastating than either sister can imagine.

Neither sister is the main character. Neither is the supporting character.
They are co-leads of identical standing — two halves of one sovereign force.

"The galaxy is going to answer for what it did."

The Trilogy

Three Books. Forty Years.

I
Book One

Blood Queens

Origin. Rise. First blood.

2359 – 2362

When their parents are murdered, two sisters inherit twin thrones at the worst possible moment. The senate moves to seize power. An empire prepares for war. And the truth behind their parents' deaths is more devastating than either can imagine.

Arc:GRIEF → SURVIVAL → POWER
II
Book Two

Queens at War

Full war. Internal collapse. Survival.

2365 – 2372

The Null Dominion launches a twelve-system invasion. Kelsey manages a wartime coalition on the edge of fracture. Lauren fights on three fronts — until the enemy takes her. The price of rescue will cost them everything they've built.

Arc:POWER → CRISIS → ENDURANCE
III
Book Three

Shadow Crowns

Conspiracy revealed. Final reckoning. Legacy.

2375 – 2399

The deep conspiracy is finally exposed. A dynastic claim threatens everything they've fought for. And in the war's final hours, Kelsey must decide what kind of victory is worth winning — while Lauren learns that restraint is the hardest battle she's ever fought.

Arc:ENDURANCE → REVELATION → LEGACY
The Velaryn Expanse

Four Powers. One Galaxy.

The Velaryn Sovereignty

47 Systems  •  18 Billion

The dominant power. Twin thrones. A dynasty beloved by its people, built on four hundred years of war survived and enemies outlasted. It does not announce itself. It does not need to.

The Null Dominion

84 Systems  •  32 Billion

The enemy. Autocratic empire. Expansion without end, ideology as justification, and a patience that has outlasted every opponent it has faced — until now.

The Tharion Collective

6 Systems  •  1.1 Billion

Democratic alliance. Master engineers. Their shield technology is unmatched in the known galaxy — and the price of their partnership is never cheap.

The Ember Reach

8 Systems  •  2.4 Billion

Tribal confederation. Fire-forged warriors. Their loyalty, once given, is absolute. Their betrayal, once earned, is final. The Velaryn sisters will need both qualities before this ends.

Kelsey Velaryn

Her Sovereign Grace, Queen of the Sovereign Council

Height

5'0" (152cm)

Age at Coronation

21 years

Personal Weapon

The Velaryn Sidearm

Bloodline Trait

Luminous Blue Irises

Domain

Senate oversight, foreign diplomacy, intelligence services, judiciary, trade, taxation, internal security.

Character

The blade in a velvet sheath. Measured and precise, Kelsey says less than she knows in virtually every interaction. Her empathic resonance is the strongest in the Velaryn bloodline in a generation — she is almost never surprised by what people want. Only by what they are willing to do to get it.

Appearance

Small-framed, fit and agile. Black hair worn naturally loose. Pale complexion, light freckles across the nose and cheekbones. Faint scar, left palm. Matte black diplomatic armor with aged gold crown insignia at the collar.

Lauren Velaryn

Her Sovereign Grace, Queen of the Sovereign Armada

Height

5'2" (157cm)

Age at Coronation

18 years

Personal Weapons

Twin Plasma Blades + Void-Rail Pistol

Bloodline Trait

Bioluminescent Shimmer

Domain

Sovereign Armada (full fleet), ground forces, military intelligence, weapons development, border security.

Character

Lauren leads with her whole body. Physically present in every room she enters — energy forward-leaning, attention total. In combat she is supernaturally focused. The bioluminescent shimmer at her temples and collarbone during peak intensity is the only external sign of the level she operates at.

Appearance

Small-framed but visibly muscular. Black hair worn naturally loose. Luminous blue irises — more pronounced than Kelsey's, intensify under stress. Combat scar across the left jaw. Tattoo on inner right wrist: "First the people, then the throne." Matte black command armor, gold crown insignia at chest.
