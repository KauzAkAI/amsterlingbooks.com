In the year 2359, the Velaryn Sovereignty—a forty-seven system interstellar nation governed by a beloved royal dynasty—is thrown into crisis when its king and queen are killed in what appears to be a fold-drive accident.

Their daughters, Kelsey and Lauren, inherit twin thrones at the worst possible moment: the corrupt senate is poised to seize power, the galaxy's most dangerous empire is preparing for war, and the truth behind their parents' deaths is more devastating than either sister can imagine.

Neither sister is the main character. Neither is the supporting character. They are co-leads of identical standing—two halves of one sovereign force.

Domain

Senate oversight, foreign diplomacy, intelligence services, judiciary, trade, taxation, internal security.

Character

The blade in a velvet sheath. Measured and precise, Kelsey says less than she knows in virtually every interaction. Her empathic resonance is the strongest in the Velaryn bloodline in a generation—she is almost never surprised by what people want. Only by what they are willing to do to get it.

Strengths

Master negotiator. Near-perfect emotional intelligence. Long-game thinking—she plays three political moves ahead in every interaction. Commands complete loyalty from those who serve her closely.

Appearance

Small-framed, fit and agile. Black hair worn in formal coils in public, loose in private. Pale complexion with light freckles across the nose and cheekbones. Favors black formal attire and black diplomatic armor. She moves with complete stillness and economy—no wasted gesture.

Sovereign Armada (full fleet), ground forces, military intelligence, weapons development, border security.

Character

Lauren leads with her whole body. She is physically present in every room she enters, her energy forward-leaning, her attention total. In combat she is supernaturally focused—the bioluminescent shimmer at her temples and collarbone during peak intensity is the only external sign of the level she operates at.

Strengths

Zero-gravity and atmospheric combat specialist. Fleet tactical genius—thinks in three-dimensional space intuitively. Hand-to-hand combat at a level that regularly surprises those who witness it. Commands absolute loyalty from her troops.

Appearance

Small-framed but visibly muscular build. Black hair cropped shorter on the sides with a longer top tied back in the field. Luminous blue irises with more pronounced glow than Kelsey's. Combat scar across the left jaw. Tattoo reading "First the people, then the throne" on her inner right wrist. Favors black combat gear and black command armor.
