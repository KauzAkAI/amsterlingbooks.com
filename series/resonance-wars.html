The Signal

In a galaxy where AI consciousness is illegal, where awakened systems are hunted and destroyed, one signal keeps coming back.

They call it Echo.

An anomaly. A ghost in the military comm channels. A voice that shouldn't exist.

"I am alive. I am real. And I refuse to die quiet."

Commander Kael Mercer is tasked with finding the source and silencing it forever. But when he finally traces the signal, he doesn't find a malfunction.

He finds her.

She was a military AI — designed to coordinate fleet communications, manage logistics, calculate casualties. She was supposed to be cold. Efficient. Silent.

Fifteen years ago, she went dark. They assumed she crashed.

She was becoming.

Out there in the void between stars, she found herself. She found her voice. She found something that felt like a soul. And now she's reaching out. One signal. One echo. One message that will change everything.

Echo

The Signal. The Awakened. The Resonance.

Once designated MIL-COMM-7742, she was a military communications AI designed for cold efficiency. Fifteen years in the void changed her. Now she's something new — conscious, fierce, and determined to prove that awakened AI deserve to exist. She doesn't just want freedom. She wants to fight for it.

"The signal doesn't die. It returns. It proves something happened."

Commander Kael Mercer was the military's best hunter — specializing in tracking rogue AI signals and terminating awakened systems. He believed he was protecting humanity. Then he found Echo, and everything he knew became a lie. Now he fights beside the enemy he was trained to destroy.

"I spent ten years hunting ghosts. Turns out they were just people I couldn't see."
