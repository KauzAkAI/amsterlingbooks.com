The Blue Tongue Agency

Two Dogs. One Agency. Zero Clues...Until Now.

When mysteries arise in the cozy town of Pawston, there's only one agency to call. Join Angus McSniff, the brilliant black Chow Chow detective, and his partner Minnie Pawsforth as they sniff out clues, chase down leads, and solve cases that leave everyone else scratching their heads.

Meet the Team

The Detectives

Pawston's finest investigative duo

Angus McSniff

Lead Detective • Black Chow Chow

With a nose that can detect a clue from three blocks away and a mind sharper than a freshly sharpened pencil, Angus leads every investigation with patience, wisdom, and an unwavering commitment to justice. His signature deerstalker hat isn't just for show — it helps him think.

Minnie Pawsforth

Junior Partner • Silver-Gray Chow Chow

Quick on her paws and even quicker with her wit, Minnie brings energy and fresh perspectives to every case. Where Angus sees patterns, Minnie sees possibilities. Together, they're unstoppable — and they always make sure to be home in time for dinner.
